# Schedule
Last check-in:
Format: [ ] open, [x] done. Sections use real dates, not "today/tomorrow". Task-size tags: defined in [[rules]] under Check-in › Task sizes. `[stated]` marks an item the user said directly.
Slugs: a row tagged `#slug` is one attempt at an ongoing thread. The background, the real deadline and the next step live on that thread's record in [[threads]] - the date here is only when the work is planned. Rows with no slug are one-and-done tasks that live here alone.
Meals (when on in [[rules]]): a dinner that has a night lives here, as a row in its week's `Dinners (tentative), week of Mon M/D:` block, which can start with a `Week adjustment:` line. The repertoire and inventories live in [[meals]].
